# Demo Notes

## What to say
- Aku-Content can generate a textbook chapter, flashcards, and quiz content from a single curriculum topic.
- The current showcase uses the Physics SS1 topic "Motion".
- The outputs are generated locally in dry-run mode and are ready to be expanded to more subjects and classes.

## Key points
- The chapter preview shows the structure of a lesson with objectives, intro, examples, and practice questions.
- The flashcards bundle supports quick revision.
- The quiz bundle provides assessment-ready questions.

## Suggested next steps
- Add more subjects such as Biology, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- Connect the generated prompts to image, audio, and video production.
- Move from dry-run mode to live provider-backed generation once credentials are ready.
